# 08. BILLING & ACCOUNTING — VARAHI GIRLS HOSTEL

## 1. Joining-Date Billing Engine

DEVI Hostels enforces an individual billing cycle tied directly to the resident's confirmed joining date, guaranteeing fair, transparent accounting without arbitrary calendar-month pro-ration penalties.

### 1.1 Cycle Standard
- **Joining Date:** 15 November
- **Monthly Rent:** ₹7,000
- **Cycle 1:** 15 November – 14 December (Due: 15 Nov)
- **Cycle 2:** 15 December – 14 January (Due: 15 Dec)
- **Cycle 3:** 15 January – 14 February (Due: 15 Jan)

### 1.2 Month-End Edge Case Policy
For residents joining on the 29th, 30th, or 31st of a month:
- Implemented in `calculateNextBillingPeriod(admissionDateStr, offset)`.
- The system establishes a fixed `billing_anchor_day = MIN(joining_day, last_day_of_billing_month)`.
- *Example:* A resident joining on March 31 will have subsequent cycles anchor to April 30, May 31, June 30, and February 28/29, without forward cumulative date drift.

## 2. Segregation of Financial Instruments

### 2.1 Advance Rent vs. Refundable Security Deposit
To preserve legal, taxation, and accounting purity, Advance Rent and Security Deposits are handled via isolated ledgers:

| Attribute | Advance Rent | Security Deposit |
|---|---|---|
| **Purpose** | Pre-payment toward future accommodation | Collateral against damage/unpaid exit dues |
| **Refundable?** | Non-refundable (Applied against future rent) | 100% Refundable upon clean checkout |
| **Ledger Storage** | `advanceLedgerStore` / `advance_balance` | `depositLedgerStore` / `security_deposit` |
| **Tax / Revenue Impact** | Recognized as earned revenue upon invoice match | Balance sheet liability (Held in escrow) |
| **Automatic Offset** | Auto-deducted from subsequent monthly recurring invoices | Never co-mingled with recurring rent |

## 3. Omnichannel Payment Allocation & Reconciliation Matrix
When a payment is received (Online or Offline):
1. **Invoice Matching:** Automatically applies against oldest open invoice balance or specific target invoice.
2. **Excess Payments:** Overpayments automatically route to `advanceLedgerStore` as an advance credit balance.
3. **Partial Payments:** Decrements `balance_due`, increments `amount_paid`, and sets status to `partially_paid`.
4. **Permanent Sequential Receipts:** Every payment generates an immutable receipt number `RCPT-[YEAR]-[SERIAL]` (e.g. `RCPT-2026-001`).
5. **Reversals:** Direct modifications or deletions of verified financial rows are prohibited. Any adjustment executes `reversePayment()` which:
   - Sets `is_reversed = true` on the original transaction.
   - Restores the unpaid invoice balance.
   - Inserts a counter-reversal ledger entry tagged with reason and authorizer ID.

## 4. Phase 3 Implemented Endpoints & Pages

### 4.1 API Endpoints
- `GET /api/financials/overview`: Central gross invoicing, collections, dues, advance, deposits, and branch metrics.
- `GET, POST /api/financials/invoices`: List filtered invoices; generate recurring invoices.
- `GET, POST /api/financials/payments`: Omnichannel payment collection ledger; records offline cash & direct UPI entries.
- `POST /api/financials/payments/reverse`: Non-destructive counter-reversal of erroneous or bounced transactions.
- `GET, POST /api/financials/deposits`: Escrow security deposit logging, damage/utility deductions, and checkout refunds.
- `GET /api/financials/advance`: Real-time audit trail of advance rent credits and monthly invoice offset debits.
- `GET /api/financials/reports`: 6 reporting pipelines:
  - `daily_collections`: Daily breakdown of revenues and transaction counts.
  - `branch_revenue`: Branch-wise comparisons and collection ratios.
  - `pending_dues`: Defaulters and overdue rent collection follow-up list.
  - `advance_balances`: Advance rent credits held across active residents.
  - `deposit_liabilities`: Total refundable escrow held by DEVI Hostels.
  - `resident_statement`: Printable, official financial account statement with letterhead.

### 4.2 Administrative & Resident Interfaces
- `/admin/finances`: Central financial KPI overview with 5-branch breakdown.
- `/admin/finances/invoices`: Recurring joining-date billing engine & bulk invoice generator.
- `/admin/finances/payments`: Collection ledger, offline recorder, and transaction reversal module.
- `/admin/finances/deposits`: Security deposit manager, inspection deduction handler, and checkout refund engine.
- `/admin/finances/reports`: Financial audit sheets and resident statement letterhead generator.
- `/resident/invoices`: Monthly rent invoices with online payment modal (UPI / Cards / Net Banking) powered by Razorpay order generation and verification.
