# 01. PROJECT OVERVIEW — VARAHI GIRLS HOSTEL

## 1. Executive Summary
**VARAHI GIRLS HOSTEL** is a premier student and working professionals accommodation network operating in **Shamshabad, Hyderabad, Telangana, India**, serving collegiate students and airport employees near Rajiv Gandhi International Airport (RGIA). The business is owned and operated by **E. BHARATH CHANDRA**.

The DEVI Hostels platform is an enterprise-grade, centralized, multi-branch hostel management ERP, resident self-service portal, admission enquiry pipeline, and automated financial accounting system.

## 4. Primary Customer Profiles

### A. College Students
- **Institutions:** Nearby engineering, degree, aviation, and management institutes in Shamshabad, Gaganpahad, and Rajendranagar.
- **Needs:** High-speed unlimited WiFi for coursework, nutritious 3-times hygienic South & North Indian food, quiet study environment, daily housekeeping, security, parent communication channels, and affordable multi-sharing room options.

### B. Airport Staff & Aviation Professionals
- **Employers:** RGIA airport operators (GMR), commercial airlines (IndiGo, Air India, SpiceJet, Akasa Air, etc.), ground handling agencies (AISATS, Çelebi Ground Handling), airport retail, customs/cargo clearing, and flight catering.
- **Needs:** 24/7 hot water, biometric security, noise insulation for rotational night-shift sleepers, round-the-clock entry protocol, quick airport commute accessibility, and transparent monthly payment tracking.

---

## 5. Technology Stack Summary

- **Frontend Application:** Next.js (App Router), React 19, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, Lucide React, Framer Motion.
- **Backend & Database:** Supabase, PostgreSQL 15+, Supabase Auth, Row Level Security (RLS), Supabase Storage.
- **Financial & Payments:** Razorpay / Cashfree API (Online UPI, Net Banking, Cards), Cash & Direct Bank Transfer verification ledger.
- **Messaging & Notifications:** WhatsApp Cloud API, Resend (Transactional Email), SMS Gateway (OTP & Emergency Alerts).
- **Hosting & Infrastructure:** Vercel (Edge/Serverless Runtime), Supabase Managed Cloud, GitHub Version Control.

---

## 6. Phased Implementation Roadmap
- **Phase 1:** Technical Foundation, Centralized Database Schema, Branch Models, Public Website, Admission Enquiry Flow, and Admin Enquiry Management.
- **Phase 2:** Resident Lifecycle, Unique DEVI Resident ID Engine, Floor/Room/Bed Capacity Tracking, Dual Authentication, and Resident Self-Service Portal.
- **Phase 3:** Joining-Date Recurring Billing Engine, Advance Rent Ledger, Refundable Security Deposit Accounting, Online Gateway + Offline UPI/Cash Reconciliation, and PDF Invoicing.
- **Phase 4:** Scheduled Cron Automations, WhatsApp/Email Payment Reminders, Escalation Matrix, Expense Tracking, Operational Analytics, and Production Launch.
