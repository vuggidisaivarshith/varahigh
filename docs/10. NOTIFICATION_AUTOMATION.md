# 10. NOTIFICATION AUTOMATION — VARAHI GIRLS HOSTEL

## 1. Multi-Channel Messaging Infrastructure

DEVI Hostels delivers automated real-time notifications across three tiers:
1. **WhatsApp Cloud API:** Primary communication channel for Indian college students and airport professionals.
2. **Transactional Email (Resend):** Formal documentation, monthly invoice delivery, and official payment receipts.
3. **SMS Gateway:** Time-critical OTP verification, admission approval alerts, and high-priority escalation notices.

---

## 2. Automated Rent Reminder & Escalation Schedule

The automated reminder cron service (`/api/cron/reminders`) scans open invoices daily and triggers reminders based on the resident's unique joining-date billing anchor:

| Timing | Channel | Template & Content |
|---|---|---|
| **Due Date - 5 Days** | WhatsApp + Email | Advance courtesy reminder: Upcoming rent invoice generated with amount & due date. |
| **Due Date - 2 Days** | WhatsApp | Payment reminder with direct 1-click UPI payment link. |
| **Due Date (0 Days)** | WhatsApp + SMS | Rent due today alert. |
| **Due Date + 2 Days** | WhatsApp | Gentle overdue notice with late-fee grace policy reminder. |
| **Due Date + 7 Days** | WhatsApp + SMS | 2nd overdue reminder; Branch Manager & Accountant notified for direct counter follow-up. |
| **Due Date + 15 Days** | WhatsApp + SMS + Email | Critical escalation alert; Owner **E. BHARATH CHANDRA** alerted for executive review. |

*Rule:* Reminders stop immediately once `invoice.status = 'paid'`. For partial settlements, notifications reflect only the remaining balance.

## 3. Implemented Notification Architecture

### 3.1 Template Catalog (`src/lib/data/notifications-repo.ts`)
- `admission_welcome`: Permanent Resident ID, assigned branch, room, bed, and self-service portal link.
- `invoice_generated`: Invoice number, billing period, balance due, and online pay link.
- `payment_reminder`: Courtesy alert with due date and 1-click UPI pay link.
- `payment_receipt`: Instant confirmation with sequential receipt number (`RCPT-2026-XXX`).
- `overdue_escalation`: Escalation notices tagged with overdue day count.
- `transfer_confirmed`: Confirmation of room or branch transfer preserving permanent Resident ID.
- `checkout_settlement`: Deposit refund and checkout signoff.
- `complaint_update`: Live status updates on hostel maintenance tickets.
- `broadcast_announcement`: Mass announcements targeted by branch code or broadcast globally.

### 3.2 Scheduled Automation Endpoints
- `GET, POST /api/cron/billing`: Automated daily billing engine generating recurring invoices 5 days before next cycle start and offsetting advance rent.
- `GET, POST /api/cron/reminders`: Daily overdue and payment reminder engine executing the -5d, -2d, 0d, +2d, +7d, +15d schedule.
- `GET, POST /api/notifications`: Dispatches single or multi-channel messages and handles branch broadcasts.
