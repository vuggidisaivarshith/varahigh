# 17. OPERATING MANUAL — VARAHI GIRLS HOSTEL

## 1. Introduction & Operational Roles

This manual provides clear standard operating procedures (SOPs) for VARAHI GIRLS HOSTEL staff and residents in Shamshabad, Hyderabad under Owner **E. BHARATH CHANDRA**.

---

## 2. Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs)

### 2.1 Owner SOP (E. BHARATH CHANDRA)
1. **Daily Overview:** Log in to `/admin` to review 5-branch overall occupancy, daily collections, and new enquiries.
2. **Escalations:** Review rent invoices overdue past 15 days in the escalation queue.
3. **Approvals:** Authorize security deposit refunds and exceptional discount/fee adjustments.

### 2.2 Branch Manager SOP
1. **Morning Lead Review:** Open `/admin/enquiries` filtered by your branch code (1 to 5). Call all `new` applicants within 2 hours.
2. **Room Tour & Status Update:** Once a prospective student or airport employee visits the branch, change status to `visited`.
3. **Admission Execution:** When rent and security deposit terms are agreed, confirm admission. Ensure room and bed are verified vacant before generating the permanent Resident ID.

### 2.3 Accountant SOP
1. **Collection Logging:** Record all physical cash payments and direct UPI transfers immediately with accurate transaction reference notes.
2. **Bank Reconciliation:** Match bank statement credits against pending direct UPI transactions before marking them verified.
3. **Monthly Invoicing:** Verify recurring billing run on residents' individual joining dates.

### 2.4 Warden SOP
1. **Check-In Verification:** Verify student college ID or airport AEP badge, collect emergency contact details, and inspect assigned bed.
2. **Complaints Management:** Log any plumbing, electrical, WiFi, or food feedback in the complaints portal and update resolution status.
3. **Curfew & Night Shifts:** Check off airport staff on night/rotational shifts for smooth 24/7 entry and quiet-hours compliance.

### 2.5 Resident SOP
1. **Self-Service Portal:** Access `/resident/login` using registered mobile number or Resident ID.
2. **Fee Payments:** Pay monthly rent before the due date via UPI or card directly through the portal to receive an instant digital receipt.
3. **Notice & Maintenance:** Submit maintenance tickets and checkout notices via the portal.
