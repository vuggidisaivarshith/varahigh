## [0.3.0] - Phase 3 Financial Management & Payment System
### Added
- **Joining-Date Billing Engine (`src/lib/data/financials-repo.ts`):**
  - Resident individual billing cycles starting on confirmed joining date (e.g. 15 Nov – 14 Dec).
  - Last-valid-day month-end safety (29th, 30th, 31st) without cumulative forward date drift.
  - Automatic invoice number generation (`INV-[YEAR]-[SERIAL]`).
  - Automatic deduction and offset of advance rent credits before computing final invoice balance due.
- **Isolated Advance & Escrow Ledgers:**
  - Real-time advance rent ledger (`advanceLedgerStore`) separate from security deposits.
  - Refundable security deposit escrow ledger (`depositLedgerStore`) with damage inspection deductions and checkout refunds.
- **Omnichannel Payment Collections & Non-Destructive Reversals:**
  - Omnichannel payment recording (`recordPayment`): Cash counter receipts, direct UPI, and bank transfers.
  - Sequential receipt number generation (`RCPT-[YEAR]-[SERIAL]`).
  - Transaction reversal mechanism (`reversePayment`): preserves original row, sets `is_reversed = true`, creates counter-adjustment record, and restores invoice balances.
- **Payment Gateway Integration (Razorpay / Cashfree):**
  - Server-side order generation (`POST /api/payments/razorpay/order`).
  - Cryptographic HMAC-SHA256 signature verification & duplicate-preventing idempotency (`POST /api/payments/razorpay/verify`).
  - Online payment modal on Resident Portal (`/resident/invoices`) for UPI, Cards, and Net Banking.
- **Admin Financial Control Hub (`/admin/finances/*`):**
  - Central Financial Dashboard (`/admin/finances`) with gross invoiced, collections, dues, and branch summaries.
  - Invoice Manager (`/admin/finances/invoices`) with recurring invoice generator.
  - Payment Collection Ledger (`/admin/finances/payments`) with offline cash/UPI recorder and reversal action.
  - Security Deposit Manager (`/admin/finances/deposits`) with escrow balances, deductions, and refunds.
  - Reporting & Audit Hub (`/admin/finances/reports`) covering daily collections, branch revenue, pending dues, advance liabilities, deposit liabilities, and printable resident account statements.
- **Verification Suite:**
  - `test-phase3-suite.ts` with 22/22 unit and regression tests passing.
  - 0 TypeScript or build errors in Next.js Turbopack compiler across all 67 routes.

## [0.2.0] - Phase 2 Resident Management & Hostel Operations
### Added
- **Room & Bed Inventory Management:**
  - Full Branch ➔ Floor ➔ Room ➔ Bed allocation hierarchy (`src/lib/data/inventory-repo.ts`).
  - Double allocation prevention logic.
  - Branch-wise vacancy summaries (`/api/inventory/vacancy-summary`).
- **Permanent DEVI Resident ID Engine:**
  - Monotonically increasing atomic ID format: `DV[BRANCH_CODE]-[FLOOR_NUMBER]-[UNIQUE_NUMBER]` (e.g. `DV1-1-00001`).
  - Preserved permanently across room/branch transfers.
- **Dual Resident Authentication:**
  - Method 1: Mobile Phone Number + OTP.
  - Method 2: Resident ID + Password.
  - Endpoint `/api/auth/resident-login` resolving both methods to identical resident profile and managing sessions.
- **10-Page Resident Portal (`/resident/*`):**
  - Dashboard (`/resident/dashboard`) with metrics: ID, category, room & bed, rent, balances, payment history.
  - Profile (`/resident/profile`), My Hostel (`/resident/my-hostel`), Invoices (`/resident/invoices`), Payments (`/resident/payments`), Advance (`/resident/advance`), Deposits (`/resident/deposits`), Digital Receipts (`/resident/receipts`), Notifications (`/resident/notifications`), Support & Maintenance Tickets (`/resident/support`).
- **Admin Management Operations:**
  - Inventory visualizer (`/admin/inventory`).
  - Central resident directory (`/admin/residents`).
  - Confirmed admission desk (`/admin/admissions`).
  - Transfers & Checkout workflows (`/admin/operations`).
